CONNECT YOUR GODADDY DOMAIN TO FORM

1. In Netlify, open your Form project → Domain management → Add a domain → Add a domain you already own. Enter your domain.
2. In GoDaddy, open your domain → DNS → DNS Records.
3. Set these records (replace YOUR-SITE with your Netlify project address):
   A       @       75.2.60.5
   CNAME   www     YOUR-SITE.netlify.app
   Edit existing records for @ and www rather than adding conflicting duplicates. Remove conflicting A/AAAA records for these names. Leave email/MX records unchanged.
4. In Netlify, make www.yourdomain.com the primary domain. Check Domain management → HTTPS after DNS updates. DNS can take up to 48 hours.

This directs your domain's main website to Form, replacing any existing website at that address.

IPHONE
After the updated site is deployed, open your final domain in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → name it Form → Add. If you already have an old shortcut, remove it and add it again to refresh the icon.
